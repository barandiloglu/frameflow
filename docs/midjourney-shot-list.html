Read first

The style bible: one look across every frame.

Two registers, and every prompt below is complete on its own. Drawn frames (the home reel and the storyboard panels) share one ink line: pencil and ink on white paper with a single amber accent, the site's own call-sheet vocabulary. Photographic frames (the cast reference and the portal demo) share a film line. Nothing in the drawn set should look like a photograph, and nothing in the photographic set goes in the reel.

--arSet per slot below. Generate at the listed ratio, never crop from a square.
--style rawTurns off Midjourney's default beautification: drawings stay as linework, photos read as film stills.
--no text, …Midjourney invents lettering on screens, cards and signage. Every prompt suppresses it; the site adds its own type on top.
--srefAfter the first approved image, paste its URL as --sref on the rest (--sw 150) to lock grain, light and colour.
--seedOptional. Reusing the seed of a keeper makes variations of the same shot instead of a new one.

Workflow

  1. Shoot Frame 001 · Brand Identity first, as a drawing. It is the largest slot and it sets the ink line for the other ten drawn frames.
  2. Pick the keeper, upscale it, copy its image URL. Add --sref <url> --sw 150 to every other prompt in this sheet.
  3. Run the other six reel cards, then the four storyboard panels, all on that --sref. The cast and portal prompts are photographic and stand apart.
  4. Upscale, crop to the exact ratio in each slot's spec, export WebP at the listed size (2× the largest measured slot).
  5. Drop files at the suggested /public paths. The reel card already blends a white-paper drawing onto either ground; nothing else to wire.

Home · / · Frame 02 “Seven scenes we'll shoot for you” · src/app/page.tsx ReelCard

The reel: seven service cards, seven storyboard frames.

These cards have no image element; the blank is the card's own top half. A photograph was tried here and read as stock: on this site photos are evidence (portfolio, gallery, cutting room) and everything else is typeset or drawn. So the reel is drawn. “Seven scenes we'll shoot for you” is future tense, and a scene not yet shot is a storyboard; the portfolio is the footage.

One file, both grounds. Generate every frame as ink on clean white paper. The card blends it with mix-blend-mode: multiply on the ivory section (dark theme) so the paper disappears, and invert + hue-rotate(180deg) then screen on the graphite section (light theme) so the same drawing prints as ivory ink on graphite with the amber accent intact. The card copy keeps the section's own ink. A mask fades the drawing out toward the lower left, so keep the subject in the upper right as every prompt asks. All seven from one --sref; shoot 001 first.
