02 / Feature

The Walnut Arch.

A dining table cut in walnut, the arched legs holding the underside of a paper lantern. Loaded lightly: a vessel, a closed monograph, the corner of a cane chair to remember the room.

Book-matched. The grain reads as a single continuous wave from end to end if you stand at the head; from the side, it is two halves of a question.
