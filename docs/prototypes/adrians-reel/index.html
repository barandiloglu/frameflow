Scene 02 · The courtyard

Built around the firepit.

Eight cabins sit around a single shared courtyard — gas grills, a working firepit, market umbrellas, a pair of Adirondack chairs each, and a short walk to the beach. Adrian's is built so the room next door becomes a neighbour by the second night.

We came in over four mornings to film it. The first morning we made seventy-two frames; the fourth, twelve.
