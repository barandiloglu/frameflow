# Adrian's Wasaga Beach — six design directions

Six FrameFlow portfolio prototypes for **Adrian's Wasaga Beach** —
a boutique 8-cabin courtyard rental on Dunkerron Avenue, three blocks
from the longest freshwater beach in the world.

The first three (01–03) lead with **brand feel** — they show the
client's voice and visual register. The next three (04–06) lead with
**substance** — they show what FrameFlow actually delivered for the
client (photography, 35-post social campaign, and the live website
at `adrianswasagabeach.ca`).

## Feel-led directions (01–03)

These three were the first round. They show the brand's tone and
visual register — what Adrian's *feels* like — but they don't
explicitly itemise what FrameFlow built. Use them when the goal is
the brand experience.

### Direction 01 — *The Postcard Stack*

> **Warmest. Most playful. Sand-yellow summer scrapbook.**

The whole page is **mail from Adrian's**. The hero is a stack of
five tilted Polaroids with masking-tape strips, beside a working
**75¢ stamp** in Live Orange and Dark Gray (perforated edges, "Wasaga
Beach · Ontario · CA · May 2026"). The photo gallery is a 12-cell
**postcards-on-corkboard grid** — each card with a brass pushpin,
hand-set caption, and randomised rotation. A **Mailbag** section
showcases the social campaign as a Seafoam strip with twelve real
posts in a 4×3 grid (perforated edges top and bottom, like a sheet
of stamps). Closes with a chunky "Send a postcard → Begin a project"
CTA.

**Use this if** the goal is **summer warmth** and the playful brand
cues that already live in the social campaign.

### Direction 02 — *The Boutique Stay*

> **Most utilitarian. Closest to a real boutique-resort booking site.**

Sticky main nav, full-bleed sunset hero, Airbnb-style booking bar,
3-column suite grid with badges and prices, location card with the
aerial photo and a pulsing pin, 6×2 Instagram feed strip, review
block with the 4.9 ★ Google score and four real five-star
testimonials. Standard scope rows. Dark "Save your cabin" CTA section.

**Use this if** the priority is **conversion** — designers,
hospitality buyers, or trade partners can scan the offering in 30s.

### Direction 03 — *The Resort Reel*

> **Most cinematic. Dark Gray surface, Live Orange embers, full-bleed.**

A single day at the resort, told as a short film: REC dot title-card
opener, then four chapters (Morning · Midday · Golden hour · Night),
each with a time-stamp title card and full-bleed scenes with monochrome
slates and italic captions. Letterboxed opener, slow scene zoom,
animated film grain. End matter: End Credits with crew table, social
thumb strip, three review star-cards, and a final dark "Roll the next
summer" slate.

**Use this if** the priority is **emotional weight and dwell time**.

## Substance-led directions (04–06)

These three were the second round, after the feedback that the feel
prototypes didn't show what FrameFlow had actually delivered. All
three present the same three deliverables (photography, social,
website) but in different organising structures — and all three
include a full browser-chrome mockup of `adrianswasagabeach.ca`
rendered with the real brand kit, real nav, real hero copy, real
two-location cards, and the "Designed by FrameFlow" footer credit.

### Direction 04 — *The Case Study*

> **Most explicit portfolio format. Section-by-section brief.**

Hero introduces three deliverables on tape. Brief band states the
ask. Then three labelled sections — **01 Photography**, **02 Social**,
**03 Website** — each with an approach card, sample work, and a
metrics row. The website section is the centrepiece: a full
browser-chrome mockup of the home page (nav, hero, two location
cards, real footer), three sub-page mini-mockups (Beach House,
Standard Cottages, Gallery), a sitemap tree of all 12 real URLs, and
a "Visit the live site →" CTA linking to adrianswasagabeach.ca. Proof
section ends with a dashed-orange callout featuring the "Designed by
FrameFlow" footer credit.

**Use this if** the priority is a **clean, readable portfolio brief**
— deliverable by deliverable, with the work shown alongside the
explanation.

### Direction 05 — *The Workbook*

> **Studio process journal. Reads like working notes.**

A six-page workbook with an index-tab nav on the right edge. Cover →
The Brief (a handwritten note from the hostess) → The Brand Kit
(annotated with margin scribbles) → Photography (contact sheet → red-X
selects → kept ✓ → final print, then all 16 finals pinned in shipped
order) → Social Campaign (the 6-template system + a calendar grid +
the post wall) → Website (sitemap card + browser-chrome home + four
sub-page mini-frames + live CTA). Closes with reviews and the
Designed-by-FrameFlow footer callout.

**Use this if** the priority is showing **how the studio works** —
the process and the working files, not just the finished deliverables.

### Direction 06 — *The Three Acts*

> **Sequential narrative deep-dive. Each deliverable as a chapter.**

A guided three-act tour. Opener title card. **Act I Photography** —
full-bleed hero plate with cinema slate, three sunset plates as a
3-up gallery, three interior plates, all 16 finals as a desaturated
contact strip. Intermission pull-quote. **Act II Social Campaign** —
the campaign as a tilted poster wall, two featured posts as
close-ups, a 4-cell stats strip. Intermission. **Act III Website** —
full browser-chrome mockup of the home page, three sub-page mini-frames,
sitemap card, live CTA. Closes with End Credits (4 reviews + crew
table + Designed-by-FrameFlow callout).

**Use this if** the priority is **dwell time and emotional pacing** —
the case-study substance presented as a story.

## How to choose

| If the priority is...                                      | Pick                         |
|------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Summer warmth, social-led, playful + on-brand              | **01 — The Postcard Stack**  |
| Conversion, scannable property cards, "looks like a site"  | **02 — The Boutique Stay**   |
| Cinema, dwell time, photography front-and-centre           | **03 — The Resort Reel**     |
| Clean portfolio brief — three deliverables, explicit       | **04 — The Case Study**      |
| Studio process — show how we work, not just the output     | **05 — The Workbook**        |
| Sequential story — each deliverable as a chapter           | **06 — The Three Acts**      |

```
                           feel-led
                              │
           01 Postcard ───────┼─────── 03 Reel
           (warm, playful)    │        (dark, cinematic)
                              │
                        ──────┼──────
                              │
                       02 Boutique
                       (functional, white)
                              │
                           feel-led
                              ┊
                           ────────
                              ┊
                           substance-led
                              │
        04 Case Study         │         06 Three Acts
        (brief, sectioned)    │         (narrative, sequential)
                              │
                        05 Workbook
                       (process journal)
                              │
                           substance-led
```

The Feel directions (01–03) and the Substance directions (04–06) can
be combined: a feel-led hero on top of a case-study body, or a
workbook structure that opens with a postcard cover. The six are
starting points, not final pages.

## Notes

- **The brand kit is locked** — Sand · Seafoam · Live Orange · Dark
  Gray + Montserrat + Quicksand. All six prototypes inherit exactly
  these colours and fonts; the differentiation is layout and tone,
  not visual identity.
- **Photo selection** drew from the studio's `_picks` and `_picks2`
  folders (the photographer's selects), filtered for the strongest
  shots across five categories: sunset (5), daytime (3), aerial
  (3 + 1 beach-context), interiors (4), property context (1).
- **Social posts** were chosen for layout variety — booking
  announcement, color headline, polaroid stack, postcard, mask
  text, quote pull, aerial breakdown — to demonstrate the campaign's
  range.
- **Reviews** are real, verbatim from Google (Pam B · Anne D ·
  Rougebella · L R, all 5/5).
- **Website mockup** content (nav, copy, addresses, phone, footer
  credit) is real, lifted from `adrianswasagabeach.ca` for accuracy.
