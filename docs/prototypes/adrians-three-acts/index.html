Act I · Runtime 02:14 · Photography

I.

The look of a place that already knew who it was.

Two shoots, six months apart. 110 frames brought back across spring sunsets and summer middays. 16 plates kept — sunset courtyard, daytime cabins, four interiors, three aerials of the property in beach-context. The photography is the spine that runs through everything else: the website hero, every social post, the postcards. This act shows the strongest plates as they were meant to be seen — full bleed, captioned, in their own room.

Act II · Runtime 02:18 · Social Campaign

II.

The posters, on a wall.

35 posts across Q2 and Q3 2026 — one summer-long conversation with the people thinking about a beach week. Six layout templates were built up front so the hostess could approve any post in 30 seconds. Postcard · Polaroid · Quote · Headline · Aerial · Mask. This act puts the campaign on a wall.
