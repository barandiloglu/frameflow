Why we are here.

Destan opened on a quiet Toronto street with a wood-fired temperament and a single product the rest of the city wasn’t serving — cağ kebabı, the rotating roast of Erzurum. Bound horizontally, turned by hand above oak embers.

I came back three times before the chef stopped looking at the camera. That was the day we made the picture above. The notebook starts here.

Dear chef,

I came back three times before you stopped looking at the camera. The first day I made eighty-two ninety-six frames; you watched all ninety-six of them. The second day I made eighty-four; you only watched the first dozen. The third day, I made one hundred and fifty.

By then you’d forgotten I was there. You pulled the lever, you reached for the long blade, you glanced at the doorway where the salt of the marinade had reached the threshold like an invitation. I made the picture → there.

What follows are notes from those four shoots. I’ve taped them in the order I think they should be read, but I left the polaroids out of order on purpose. a saga is allowed to skip ahead.

Thank you for the bread.
