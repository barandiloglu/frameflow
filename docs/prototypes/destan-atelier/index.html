The brief was quiet. Photograph the fire honestly, and the rest will follow.

Destan opened in twenty twenty-four with a single signature: the rotating roast of Erzurum, bound horizontally to a hand-turned spit and read at the moment of darkest crust. We were brought on for the long story — a season of stills, a season of short films, and the slow social work of building an audience that arrives hungry.

We moved across four shoots in the kitchen and kept fifteen plates. The work below is the edit. Every frame stands on its own. Every paragraph stands beside one frame.
