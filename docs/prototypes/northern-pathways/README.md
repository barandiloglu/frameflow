# Northern Pathways Immigration — twelve design directions

Twelve FrameFlow portfolio prototypes for **Northern Pathways
Immigration Consulting** — a Toronto regulated immigration practice
led by an RCIC-IRB.

> **Direction locked → Aviation.** After seeing the first ten
> directions, Baran picked **The Departure Board (09)** as the
> register he wants to ship. The next two prototypes (11–12) extend
> that aviation lineage into adjacent registers — the editorial
> magazine carried in the seatback, and the captain's private
> logbook signed at the end of every flight. **All three thread
> the brand DNA values** (Ethical Guidance · Personalised Service ·
> Transparency · Knowledge & Expertise · Care & Lived Experience)
> explicitly through the narrative. The other 7 prototypes (01–08,
> 10) remain in the catalogue as alternative chassis.

## Frame-led directions (01–03)

Round one. Different aesthetic frames, all institutional, all
honouring the locked palette. **They paired Montserrat with serif
voices that aren't part of the brand kit** — so they're useful as
reference for structure but not as final-form prototypes.

### 01 · The Dossier (frame: legal/regulatory)

Court-brief format. Roman-numeral sections, notarised cover stamp,
counsel-of-record sidebar with the principal's photo and the
RCIC/CAPIC/Queen's seals. Three exhibits (website · calculator ·
draws). Pull-quote from the brand voice + four credential seals.
Filed-in-good-faith end.

### 02 · The Console (frame: product page)

Stripe / Linear product-page idiom. Hero is a **live CRS calculator
console** with category bars and a PDF download. Three product
cards (CRS · FSWP · Latest Draws), split FSWP eligibility checker
with green verdict, live data table for draws, dark band with social
tile grid.

### 03 · The Bilingual Edition (frame: broadsheet newspaper)

Newspaper masthead, three-column front-page splash with drop-cap +
Turkish dispatch column, dual-language § B brief side by side,
exhibit cards with mockups, dark op-ed band with Burcu's portrait
card, social column grid, market-listings palette + scope.

## Concept-led directions (07–08)

Round three, built with the frontend-design skill. Strict on the
brand lock (Montserrat only, four colours), but pushed to bigger
conceptual ambition. The expressive range here comes from
**concept** and **typographic dynamic range** — not from added
fonts.

### 07 · The Atlas

> **The brand is "Northern Pathways" — so we render the engagement as an actual cartograph.**

Sheet I of an atlas, signed and scaled. A 16:9 SVG map plate fills
the viewport: a hairline coordinate grid (axes A·B·C·D / I·II·III·IV),
six concentric topographic contour rings centred on the firm,
**five plot points with crosshair markers and coordinate labels**
(*The Brief* at A·IV, *The Website* at B·II, *The Calculator* at
C·II — centre, *The Draws* at D·I, *The Feed* at D·IV), and route
lines connecting them with **CRS-distance annotations** (+50 CRS
via French · +12 via FSWP · +0 live · +248 IG · CRS > 504 → ITA).
Top-right: a **proper compass rose** with the maple-leaf logo as
the pointer. Below the chart: a 5-cell index strip linking each plot
point to its waypoint expansion. Each waypoint expansion is a real
mockup — the website, the calculator, the draws feed, the
@kanadadayeniyasam grid.

Closing folio reads "Surveyed by FrameFlow" with sheet number, scale
(1:248,000), and date. The corner labels and the publication stamp
imitate a real navigational chart's chrome.

**Use this if** the priority is **proof that the brand has a strong
metaphor** — and that the studio took it seriously enough to plot
the work cartographically.

### 08 · The Manifesto

> **One typeface, asked to do everything — at sizes from 10px to 320px.**

Nine spreads, each numbered with a folio (A through I), each holding
a single declaration set in Montserrat at maximum scale. The locked
tagline opens at **320px** filling the viewport (`Guiding your
*Canadian* immigration journey with *clarity*`). Spread B sets the
year-in-figures at 220px-each on a dark band. Spreads C–E pair giant
declarations ("**We file plainly**", "**We charge transparently**",
"**We speak two languages**") with **small inline browser-frame
figures** dwarfed by the type — the website hero with Burcu and the
service icons, the CRS calculator with its 487/1200 readout, the
@kanadadayeniyasam grid. Spread F is a **giant 504 CRS poster** for
the latest healthcare draw. Spread G is a full-bleed pull quote in
italic Montserrat 500. Spread H is a restrained scope table. The
closing spread is a **page-spanning red bleed** with the words
"Designed & developed by Frame Flow" set at 320px, italic on
"Frame Flow", with a charcoal punctuation mark.

The whole prototype is a proof: that the brand's typography lock
isn't a limitation — it's an instrument capable of an entire range,
from 9px marginalia to 320px set-pieces, all in one typeface.

**Use this if** the priority is **art-direction confidence** —
showing that the studio understands the brand well enough to refuse
to add fonts even when the design seems to ask for them.

### 09 · The Departure Board

> **The lineage: Pentagram-Heathrow signage. Otl Aicher-Frankfurt isotype. Solari split-flap displays.**

A working international terminal for an immigration practice. The
top-of-page **sign bar** carries terminal number, hub code (YYZ),
captain (Burcu), and a live ET clock. The marquee section is an
authentic **Solari split-flap board** — black background with aged-
amber digits, scanline overlay, six rows showing the six most recent
IRCC draws as flights (NP347 / NP346 / NP345…) with **flight number,
programme, destination route, gate, category, equipment code (CRS-
504 / CRS-379 etc.) and status**. Status pills colour-code the only
red on the board: a **blinking BOARDING** flag for the current draw.
Each cell flips into place on page load with a staggered split-flap
animation.

Below: the five services rendered as **real boarding passes** —
each with carrier prefix (NP), flight number, IATA-style route
(YYZ → PR / YYZ → FAM / YYZ → CIT), departure time, equipment
code, a perforated separator with notch holes punched through it,
and a detachable stub showing **gate number, boarding zone tied to
CRS, and seq number**. Captain Burcu gets a **standalone airline ID
card** with red stripe header, ID photo (1:1.05 ratio), licence
metadata (RCIC-IRB / CICC / CAPIC / Queen's), and a CAPT rank in
the top-right.

The CRS calculator becomes a **Flight Calculator** — pre-flight
check at Gate D, with a flight indicator on the right reading 487
in aged-amber on charcoal, and a "Confirm itinerary · ↓ PDF
manifest" CTA. The Instagram grid becomes the **Inflight Magazine**
(volume I · issue 248). The website mockup becomes Connecting
Documentation at Gate F. Closing: a black callsign card on red
that reads "Roll for *departure*. The captain holds the manifest."

**Use this if** the priority is **on-brand wit** — taking the
client's actual category (immigration = literal departure) and
rendering it through institutional aviation-corporate-identity
precision, without slipping into clip-art travel cliché.

### 10 · The Specimen

> **The lineage: Klim Type Foundry specimen books. Hoefler & Co catalogues. Commercial Type sample sheets.**

A 10-plate **type-foundry catalogue** for a brand that holds itself
to one typeface and four colours. Within that lock, an entire
range — and the catalogue documents it.

- **Cover** — `Montserrat × #B92025.` set at 280px. Edition
  number, plate count, foundry stamp.
- **Index** — 10 numbered plates listed with page numbers.
- **Plate 01 · The Colour** — `#B92025` as a 156px hex specimen on
  a maple-red field, paired with **full Pantone-grade metadata**:
  RGB / CMYK / HSL / Lab / Pantone approx. / WCAG ratios on white
  and on charcoal. Tint range as four swatches.
- **Plate 02 · The Type** — Montserrat at every weight (300 · 400
  · 500 · 600 · 700 · 800 · 900) with sample sentences and use
  notes: *captions / body / subhead / labels / ctas / headlines /
  display set pieces*.
- **Plate 03 · The Italic** — Montserrat italic 500 alone, set as
  the brand's emphasis voice at 156px display, with metadata card
  ("never bold-italic", "tracking -0.025em").
- **Plate 04 · The Pairings** — Four colour combinations as 4:5
  type specimens (Charcoal × Red on Ink / Paper × Italic on Red /
  Ink × Red on Paper / Ink × Red on Off-white).
- **Plates 05–07 · In Use** — The website, calculator, and (placeholder)
  Instagram feed shown in proper foundry-grade plate format: a
  browser-chrome figure on the left, a **"specimen card" caption on
  the right** with caliper-precise typography metadata (Heading set
  in Montserrat 800 / clamp(28,3.4vw,40) / -0.02em / etc.).
- **Plate 08 · The Marginalia** — Six small-text specimens: buttons
  (primary/secondary), pills (soft/solid), rules (1px/1.5px/4px-red),
  marginalia, numerical scores, the lockup-airline pattern.
- **Plate 09 · The Lockup** — The NP wordmark on paper and the
  maple-leaf mark on charcoal, side by side.
- **Plate 10 · Colophon** — End-of-catalogue, colophon copy
  ("Set in Montserrat by Julieta Ulanovsky..."), edition metadata.

Every plate carries **corner registration marks**, a numbered
plate header, and a foundry-grade caption strip at the bottom
(`P.05 · Plate 05 · Use · website hero`). Reads like a real foundry
catalogue, sells like a portfolio.

**Use this if** the priority is **showing the studio understands
brand systems** — building a piece that respects and *celebrates*
the lock the client already imposed. The most design-literate of
the ten.

## ★ Aviation lineage (09 · 11 · 12) — recommended

The chosen direction. All three honour the locked typeface and
palette, render the engagement through aviation/airline corporate-
identity, and **explicitly thread the brand DNA values through
the narrative.**

### 09 ★ The Departure Board

> **Public-terminal register.**

The marquee piece. Solari split-flap board for IRCC draws.
Boarding passes for the five services. Captain Burcu on an airline
ID. Flight Calculator for CRS. Inflight Magazine and Connecting
Documentation as supporting sections. *See full description above.*

### 11 ★ The In-Flight Magazine

> **Editorial register — the magazine carried in the seatback.**

*Yeni Yaşam* — "new life" — Vol. I, Issue №019. Magazine cover with
five numbered cover lines, barcode, ISSN. Inside front cover with
masthead (Burcu = Editor in Chief). Table of contents with editorial
blurbs and page numbers. **Letter from the Captain** with drop-cap
and signed scribble. **Brand DNA spread** — the five values
rendered as five themed feature themes, each tied to a specific
deliverable.

Long-form features on the website ("Touch Down") and CRS calculator
("The Approach") with proper editorial deck, two-column drop-cap
body, and embedded browser-frame figures with foundry-grade caption
strips. **Photo essay spread on a dark page** — `Yeni bir yaşam.` —
where the social posts live as a 12-tile editorial layout (8-column
grid with varied spans) ready for real exports. Centre pull-quote.
Red-bleed back cover signed *"Designed by Frame Flow."* Every page
carries the running head, page number, and folio. Reads like an
actual published magazine.

**Use this if** the priority is **publication weight** — making
the engagement feel like a season's worth of work edited and
published, not a portfolio dump.

### 12 ★ The Captain's Logbook

> **Document register — the captain's signed private record.**

Leather-bound cover with gilt foil and corner brackets, debossed
"BA" monogram with RCIC subtitle. Inside pages have **hole-punch
dots in the left margin, red ledger rule line, and faint horizontal
guides** like ruled paper. The frontispiece is Burcu's pilot
licence card with photo, licence number, signature, and rank.

**Operational Doctrine** — the brand DNA values reframed as the
airline's five operating articles. Each article cites the flight
it governs ("Doctrine 03 → Flight NP-247 · Calculator").

Then the **flight log proper** — four entries, one per deliverable,
each with a ruled ledger row (Date · Route · Hours · Weather ·
Equipment · Status), captain's narrative notes with handwritten-feel
italic Montserrat marginalia in the margin, an embedded mockup of
the deliverable, and a **signed signoff** at the bottom of the page
("B. Akyol" scribble + "Cleared" stamp).

Flight 03 (Social Programme) becomes a **photo album spread** —
nine "in-flight photographs" pasted in with **rotated photo corners**
and **manila photo-corner clips**, each with an italic editorial
caption and a "Ph. 03.7 · placeholder" reference. **Tile-by-tile
ready to swap when the marketing team supplies the real exports.**

Closes on **year-end totals** (8,200+ hours) and a **captain's
certification page** with a circular stamp ("CERTIFIED · Sec. D ·
2026"), three signature scribbles (Captain · stamp · Studio of
Record), and a sealed back cover.

**Use this if** the priority is **intimacy and authority at once** —
showing that the engagement was not just signage but a regulated
counsel's signed work, kept in a private logbook the way a
commercial pilot keeps theirs.

## How to choose

| If the priority is...                                            | Pick                          |
|------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **★ Recommended (locked direction)** — public terminal           | **09 — The Departure Board**  |
| **★ Editorial weight** — magazine carried in seatback            | **11 — The In-Flight Magazine** |
| **★ Intimate authority** — captain's signed logbook              | **12 — The Captain's Logbook** |
| Conceptual ambition — the brand metaphor, plotted                | 07 — The Atlas                |
| Art-direction confidence — type-only at maximum scale            | 08 — The Manifesto            |
| Brand-systems literacy — foundry catalogue treatment             | 10 — The Specimen             |
| Faithful, plain rebuild of the actual deliverables               | 04 — The Walkthrough          |
| Proving omnichannel — web AND social, one voice                  | 05 — Side by Side             |
| Reportorial gravitas, year-of-work review                        | 06 — The Annual Report        |
| Legal/regulatory frame (uses non-brand serif)                    | 01 — The Dossier              |
| Product-led tech pitch (uses non-brand serif)                    | 02 — The Console              |
| Multilingual diaspora narrative (uses non-brand serif)           | 03 — The Bilingual Edition    |

Recommended: pick from the **★ aviation trio (09 · 11 · 12)**.
The three occupy adjacent registers and could be hybridised — the
Departure Board's chrome at the top of an Annual Report-style body,
or the Logbook's flight entries embedded in the Magazine's photo
essay spread.
