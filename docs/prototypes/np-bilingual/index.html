Front Page · The Story Filed by FrameFlow

Guiding a journey
in two languages.

A multilingual website, a calculator suite, a live draws feed, and a Turkish-language Instagram for the Canada-bound diaspora.

FrameFlow was retained by Northern Pathways Immigration Consulting to ship three things: a marketing site that reads as well in Turkish as it does in English, a pair of regulatory-grade calculators (CRS and FSWP) the firm could put a counsel’s name to, and a live tracker indexing every IRCC Express Entry draw since 2015.

The brief was short. A regulated immigration practice cannot afford to be loose with numbers, and most of its prospective clients land on the site through Turkish-language search and word-of-mouth. The work had to feel institutional in both registers without losing the warmth that brings people to the door.

The site, at northernpathways.ca, runs at /en and /tr with first-class language detection. The home view holds the firm’s tagline in italic serif — guiding your journey with clarity — and every other line is set in Montserrat, the locked typeface.

The CRS calculator follows IRCC’s 2025 grid, including the recent removal of arranged-employment points and the return of category-based draws. The FSWP eligibility checker is a binary gate at 67/100; both finish on a one-click PDF that a regulated counsel can sign.
