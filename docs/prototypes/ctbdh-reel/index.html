A note on the network

Ten businesses, one shared story.

The Canada Türkiye Business Development Hub is a network of Turkish-Canadian small and medium enterprises — founders who built their companies in Toronto, Concord and across the GTA, and who lend each other capital, contacts, and confidence.

FrameFlow filmed ten of them, one screening at a time. What follows is the programme — runtimes, sectors, and synopses for each story we filed.
