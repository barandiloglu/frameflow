A note on the work

A season for Canapy Furniture — Toronto's contract-grade studio on Davenport Road.

We were brought on for the long story. Four shoots in the Davenport Showroom and one private home in the Annex. Photography, films, social, and the small bright work of ads.

The Canapy brief was a sentence: photograph the room honestly. Daylight only — no fill, no flash. Four hundred and twelve frames came back; thirteen were kept.
