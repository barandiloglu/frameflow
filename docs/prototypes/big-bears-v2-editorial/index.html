01 · The brief

A spud with a personality.

Big Bears arrived with a single product — the loaded baked potato — and a question: how do you make a humble starch feel like a destination?

We built a mascot-led identity warm enough for kids and loud enough for late-night cravings. The bear earned the shades. The wordmark earned the size. The wrapper turned every ingredient on the menu into a typographic pattern that sells the brand from a block away.

Then we shot the food.
