Feature № 01 · Letter from the Captain

The view from the cockpit.

Most of our prospective clients board through Turkish-language search and word-of-mouth. They are looking for two things at once: warmth and authority. A regulated immigration consultancy must be both. This issue of Yeni Yaşam documents an engagement that took those two requirements seriously and shipped them as one piece of work.

FrameFlow Studio joined us in spring 2025 with one brief: to make the firm findable, bookable, and trustworthy in two languages. They built three things. A multilingual marketing site at northernpathways.ca. A pair of regulator-grade calculators — the Comprehensive Ranking System and the Six Selection Factors — that finish on a one-click PDF I can sign as counsel of record. And a Turkish-language Instagram, @kanadadayeniyasam, that mirrors every IRCC draw to the diaspora the morning it lands.

What follows is one issue of one season. Five values, three deliverables, two languages, one typeface. The footer credit on every page reads Designed and developed by Frame Flow.

Feat. № 03

A long-form feature

Touch Down — northernpathways.ca, rebuilt.

12 routes · EN+TR
Live · since 2026

A multilingual marketing site that opens in two languages and asks for the right thing first. The home view leads with Burcu Akyol's name, the firm's credentials, and a single CTA — Book a Consultation.

The site is served at /en and /tr with first-class language detection. A single book a consultation CTA appears on every route. Five service pages map to the firm's five practice areas: Economic Immigration, Family Sponsorship, Temporary Residence, PR Card & PRTD, Citizenship.

The architecture earns its keep. Each service page terminates at one of two places — the calculator (if eligibility is the question) or the onboarding form (if the question has been answered). Every page links to a consultation, a calculator, or both.

The brand DNA shows up in the small choices. Burcu's headshot in the hero answers Personalised Service. The fees page (with prices listed up front) answers Transparency. The footer credit on every page answers Knowledge & Expertise: Design & development by Frame Flow.

Feat. № 04

A long-form feature

The Approach — a CRS in 15 fields.

15 fields · 6 categories
Bilingual · PDF

A regulated Comprehensive Ranking System calculator at the centre of the engagement. Honours IRCC's 2025 grid — including the removal of arranged-employment points — and finishes on a one-click PDF.

Express Entry is a number game. The applicant has a CRS score, the IRCC has a draw cutoff, and the gap between them is the work of an immigration practice. The calculator we built lets a client see that gap before their first consultation.

It runs in two languages with full IRCC parity. Every input is tooltipped — not as a marketing flourish, but as a regulator-grade clarification. NCLC versus CLB. Provincial nominee versus federal stream. Arranged employment status under the new 2025 rules.

The PDF is the artefact. Counsel can sign it, the client can take it to a consult, and the firm can use it as the basis for an eligibility memo. The Six Selection Factors checker (FSWP eligibility, 67/100 cutoff) sits on the same stack and exports the same PDF format.
