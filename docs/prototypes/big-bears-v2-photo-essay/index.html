Chapter 01 The brief

A spud with a personality.

Big Bears arrived with a single product — the loaded baked potato — and a question: how do you make a humble starch feel like a destination?

The mascot earned the shades. The wordmark earned the size. The wrapper turned every ingredient on the menu into a typographic pattern that sells the brand from a block away. Then we shot the food.
