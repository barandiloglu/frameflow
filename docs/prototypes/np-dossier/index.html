Section I · The Matter

The brief, in two paragraphs.

Engaged
Spring 2025

A regulated immigration consultancy needed to be findable, bookable, and trustworthy in two languages.

Northern Pathways is led by a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) and adjunct professor. Most of the firm’s prospective clients arrive through Turkish-language search and word-of-mouth in the diaspora. The firm wanted to keep that warmth — but speak with the authority a regulatory body requires.

We were retained for three things: the website at northernpathways.ca, the in-house tools (a CRS calculator, an FSWP eligibility checker, a live Express Entry draws tracker), and ongoing social on @kanadadayeniyasam. All three speak the same quiet, measured voice the firm files briefs in.

IIExhibit Two

Web Application · Calculators & Tools

A pair of regulatory-grade calculators
that produce a defensible PDF.

northernpathways.ca/en/crs-calculator · /en/fswp-calculator

2 calculators
+ PDF export

Two calculators — the Comprehensive Ranking System (Express Entry pool) and the Six Selection Factors (FSWP eligibility cutoff). Both honour IRCC’s 2025 rules, including the recent removal of arranged-employment points and the return of category-based draws. Each finishes with a generated PDF the client can take to a consult.

We built them in-app, in both languages, with proper tooltips on every line. The total is a real CRS or FSWP number, not an estimate-of-an-estimate.

  • CRS rules current to 2025 (no AE points)
  • FSWP cutoff 67/100 verified live
  • Bilingual labels & tooltips · EN/TR
  • One-click PDF export · jsPDF + html2canvas

Live Feed · Latest Express Entry Draws

Every IRCC draw, indexed from 2015 to today, with the invitation count, CRS minimum, programme, and category tagged. The feed is what calls clients back to the site — it’s the only page on the firm’s domain that updates without a campaign behind it.

On @kanadadayeniyasam (“Yeni yaşam” — “new life”), the same draws are summarised in Turkish on draw day, with the firm’s commentary on what each programme means in plain language.
