Baked Potato

A complete brand build for a single-product Toronto counter — mascot logo, typographic packaging, full menu system, and food photography.

Plate I — XV FrameFlow Studio
Part oneI.

A spud with a personality.

Big Bears arrived with a single product — the loaded baked potato — and a question: how do you make a humble starch feel like a destination? The answer turned out to be a mascot, four colors, two typefaces, and one extraordinarily loud wrapper.

The bear earned the shades. The wordmark earned the size. The wrapper turned every ingredient on the menu into a typographic pattern that sells the brand from a block away. Then we shot the food.

What follows is a record of fifteen plates: the mark in primary form, the four-color palette, two typefaces, twelve photographic stills from the line, three menu spreads, and the wrapper at full bleed. The work, in order.
