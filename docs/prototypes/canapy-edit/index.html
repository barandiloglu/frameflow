The brief

Photograph the room honestly.

ClientCanapy Furniture
LocationDavenport Rd · Toronto
Surface4,500 sq ft
AudienceTrade · designers

Canapy was opened on Davenport Road with a single signature: contract-grade pieces, European-inspired, photographed at the same patience with which they are made.

The brief was four paragraphs in writing and one sentence in conversation — photograph the room honestly. No fill, no flash, no attempt to flatter the wood out of its own grain. Daylight only, on the room's tempo.

We were brought on for the long story. A season of stills. A season of short films cut for social and the Trade landing pages. A small budget of paid placements. The slow work of a calendar planned monthly with the studio.

The work

Four shoots, one quiet kitchen.

Shoot · 02Feb 24 · Plates
Shoot · 03Mar 14 · Cane
Shoot · 04Apr 05 · Trade

Each shoot has a tempo. The first morning the room watches the camera; by the third, it stops looking. We made four hundred and twelve frames; we kept thirteen.

The pieces are not new. They are the pieces a Canapy showroom has always carried — the Cane Lounge, the Walnut Arch, the Saddle Headboard, the Bouclé Sectional. The work was to make them recent again: the same forms, the same light, an honest camera.

Six short films were cut in the same edit. Soft handheld. No music — the room is the soundtrack. 9:16 native; 1:1 cut-downs supplied for the feed and paid placements.

The result

An archive that earns its keep.

Stills13 archival
Films6 cut
Sizes9:16 · 1:1 · 16:9

An imagery archive sized for every surface — 9:16 for Reels, 1:1 for the feed, 16:9 for trade decks — all delivered through the Trade Network portal the day this entry is published.

Calendar planned monthly with the studio. Reels and carousels in a single voice. Paid placements built from the same shoots, sized to the room and routed through the Trade landing pages for higher-intent inbound leads.

Next quarter we are bringing the camera to a hospitality install in Yorkville. Until then, the pieces above are on the floor at Davenport Road through the season.
