Page 03 · Letter from the Studio

— A note from FrameFlow —

What we set out to do.

Northern Pathways came to us in the spring of 2025 with a sentence: a regulated immigration consultancy needed to be findable, bookable, and trustworthy — in two languages. The brief was short. The standard, less so. A Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant signs every word the firm publishes, and we were retained to build the surfaces that voice would speak from.

We shipped three things this year. The website at northernpathways.ca, served at /en and /tr with first-class language detection and twelve routes per locale. A pair of in-house regulatory-grade calculators — the Comprehensive Ranking System estimator and the Federal Skilled Worker Six Selection Factors checker — both finishing on a one-click PDF the principal can sign. And a Turkish-language Instagram handle, @kanadadayeniyasam, mirroring every IRCC draw and every consult-day takeover to the diaspora that brings most of the firm’s prospects.

Below, the figures that came back, three chapters of deliverables, and the year ahead.
