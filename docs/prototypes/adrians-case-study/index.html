The Brief

Make us findable.

Adrian's runs two locations in Wasaga Beach — the Suites at Beach Area 1 and the Resort at Beach Area 3 — eight cabins between them, plus a beach house. The brief was practical: make us findable, make us bookable, make the Google reviews matter.

We wrote three answers — a season of photography, a 35-post social campaign, and a booking-first website. Below: what each one looks like, how it landed, and why it works.

01

Deliverable · 01 / 03

Photography — two shoots, sixteen plates.

110frames · 16 kept

Approach

Two shoots across spring and summer. Daylight only on the morning shoot for clean cabin facades; sunset + golden hour on the evening shoot for the courtyard, firepit, and door numbers — Adrian's strongest visual asset.

Aerials added in midday to show the property's relationship to Beach Area 1 and Beach Area 3. Interiors photographed last, after the housekeepers had set the rooms.
