This album catalogues one season's work by FrameFlow Studio for Northern Pathways Immigration Consulting — a regulated practice led by an RCIC-IRB. Five plates: a service-issue commemorative set, a first-day cover for the website, a customs declaration form for the calculator, a diaspora series collecting the season's social posts as mounted commemorative stamps, and a postmark plate cancelling the engagement against the firm's five doctrines.

Stamps are mounted with their original perforated edges intact. Postmarks are not retouched. Each catalogue number maps to a doctrine in the firm’s operating manual. Filed in good faith, in two languages, in one typeface.

Colophon — filed in good faith.

This album catalogues one season's work for Northern Pathways Immigration Consulting, prepared by FrameFlow Studio in Toronto, MMXXVI.

Set in Montserrat in weights 300 to 900, with a single italic at 500. Inks: charcoal #2C2B2B, paper white, off-white #F9F9F9, and a single saturated accent — maple red, #B92025. Mounted on aged cardstock, perforated to 11½ on all four sides.

Catalogued under the NP / 2026 / Vol. I series. Each stamp's denomination keys to the firm's CRS or FSWP scoring; each postmark cancels the engagement against a doctrine in the operating manual. If a stamp shows up again next year, you'll know it's been re-issued.
