— Reel II · Box office —

Buy a ticket at the box office.

The CRS calculator runs the front-of-house. 15 fields, 6 categories, rules current to IRCC's 2026 grid — including the removal of arranged-employment points. Every transaction prints a signed PDF stub the door staff (counsel) can tear at admission.

Festival rule: no entry without a stub. If the calculator says you can board the flight, the box office prints the ticket and the captain signs it.
