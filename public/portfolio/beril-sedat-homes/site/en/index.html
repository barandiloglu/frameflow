Frequently Asked

Common Questions

What makes a boutique brokerage different?

Selective representation, senior-level attention on every file, and a network of trusted partners — staging, photography, mortgage, legal — built over fifteen years in the GTA. You're not a number in a pipeline.

What is the 2026 HST rebate for new construction homes?

Qualifying new-construction homes under $1M may be eligible for a 13% HST rebate as of 2026. We guide pre-construction investors through eligibility, timing, and documentation.

Which neighbourhoods do you specialize in?

Our primary micro-markets are South-East Oakville, Humber Bay Shores, North York (St. Andrew–Windfields), the Distillery District, Richmond Hill, and Vaughan/Markham.

Do you work with pre-construction investors?

Yes — pre-construction strategy is one of our specialties. We help investors evaluate VIP launches, assignment opportunities, and long-horizon plays across the GTA.
