Before You Reach Out

A few questions answered.

How quickly will you respond?

Within a few hours during business days. Evenings and weekends, by the next morning. If something is time-sensitive, please call directly.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. The first conversation is always complimentary — whether you're buying, selling, or simply exploring. We believe the right relationship starts with a real conversation, not a contract.

Do you work outside the GTA?

Our primary expertise is the Greater Toronto Area. For clients moving in or out of the GTA, we have trusted referral partners across Canada and select international markets.

Can we meet in person?

Yes — at one of our private offices, at a property of interest, or somewhere quiet of your choosing. Private consultations are available by appointment, including evenings and weekends.
