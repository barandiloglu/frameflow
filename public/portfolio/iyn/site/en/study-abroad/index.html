· · · ·Process

From application to graduation, we walk it with you.

At IYN the journey moves through five stages — one at a time, in order, tracked. A dedicated counsellor for the student and a clear timeline for the family.

01
Discovery
University research, country fit analysis and programme mapping. Build a shortlist aligned with the student's goals.
02
Strategy
Exam plan (SAT / AP / IB / IELTS), CV structuring, academic portfolio and extracurricular strategy.
03
Application
Common App, UCAS, motivation letters, academic references — personal statement revisions with an editor.
04
Visa
Visa filing, financial documents, biometric appointment prep and a mock consulate interview.
05
Settlement
Housing, local bank account, insurance, phone line and the first-week orientation — you don't land alone.
