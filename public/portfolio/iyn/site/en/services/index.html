Standardized Exam · University

SAT

Scholastic Assessment Test
Overview

Run by the College Board, the SAT is the standardized exam most often required by US universities at the application stage. Two main modules — Reading & Writing and Math — delivered in a fully digital format.

Exam Length
2H 14M · DIGITAL
Scoring
400 - 1600
Who It's For
GRADES 10-12
Recommended Start
6+ MONTHS AHEAD
What's Included
  • Reading & Writing module
  • Math (Calculator allowed)
  • Adaptive — questions scale to performance

1 - 5 SCALE

GRADES 10-12

  • Score 4-5 = university credit
