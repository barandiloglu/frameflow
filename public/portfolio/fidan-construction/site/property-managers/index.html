FAQ

Quick answers

Is the walkthrough actually free?

Yes — completely free, no obligation. We use it to give you an accurate quote and to see if we’re the right fit. About 30 minutes on site.

What areas of Ottawa do you cover?

Ottawa metro and surrounding areas — Kanata, Nepean, Orleans, Barrhaven, Gatineau. If you’re within ~50 km of downtown Ottawa, we’ll come to you.

Do you handle insurance claims for water damage?

Yes. We document the damage, work directly with adjusters, and provide the paperwork your insurer needs.

How fast can you turn over a vacant unit?

Most standard turnovers (paint, patch, clean) take 3–5 business days. Larger or damaged units take longer; we’ll commit to a timeline in the quote.

Are you licensed for asbestos work in Ontario?

Yes. We operate in compliance with Ontario Regulation 278/05 — the provincial standard for asbestos testing, containment, and removal.

Can we get a single quote for multiple buildings?

Yes — we work with property management companies on portfolio-level pricing. Mention this on the form and we’ll structure the proposal accordingly.
