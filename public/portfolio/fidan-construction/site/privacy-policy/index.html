Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026 · Last updated: April 28, 2026

Fidan Construction (“Fidan Construction,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit fidanconstruction.com, contact us through any form (including forms shown on Facebook or Instagram ads), or otherwise interact with our services.

This policy is written in line with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applies to all individuals who provide us with personal information.

1. Information we collect

We collect personal information you give us directly when you fill out a form, request a quote, book a walkthrough, or contact us by phone or email. This typically includes:

  • Identifying information: your name, role or job title, and the company you represent.
  • Contact information: phone number, email address, and (optionally) the best time to reach you.
  • Project information:the type of service you're requesting (painting, drywall, water damage, asbestos, turnover), the size of your portfolio or building, your timeline, and any details you choose to share.
  • Site information: the address of the building or unit, only if you share it with us during a quote or walkthrough.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit our website, including your device type, browser, IP address, and how you arrived at our site (for example, from a Meta ad). See Cookies and tracking for details.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule walkthroughs, quotes, or service appointments.
  • Provide the construction, painting, drywall, water damage restoration, asbestos, or turnover services you request.
  • Communicate with you about your project (status updates, scheduling, invoices).
  • Improve our services, website, and customer experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including Ontario Regulation 278/05 (asbestos).
  • Measure the performance of our advertising and outreach.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. When we share information

We share personal information only when necessary, and only with parties who have a need to know it:

  • Service providers who help us run our business — for example, email delivery, scheduling tools, and hosting providers. They are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for the services they perform for us.
  • Insurance adjusters or regulators when relevant to a claim, restoration job, or compliance check (and only with your knowledge).
  • Legal authorities if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.
  • Business successorsin the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of Fidan Construction's business.

4. Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Lead Ads

We advertise on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) to reach property managers, building managers, and property management companies in the Ottawa region.

If you fill out a form attached to one of our ads — known as a Meta Lead Form or Instant Form — Meta collects the information you submit and shares it with us so we can follow up. Meta's collection and processing of your information at that point is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy. Once we receive your information from Meta, our handling of it is governed by this policy.

We may also use Meta's tools, including the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, to measure the effectiveness of our ads and to show ads to people similar to our existing customers (lookalike audiences). These tools may receive limited information about your interactions with our site or ads, such as the pages you viewed and whether you submitted a form. The information sent through these tools is hashed (one-way encrypted) before transmission so that direct identifiers like your email or phone number cannot be read in transit.

Your control over Meta ads: You can adjust how Meta uses your information for advertising in your Facebook Ad Preferences or by opting out of interest-based advertising on the AdChoices Canada site.

5. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for essential site functions, analytics, and advertising measurement. Specifically, we may use:

  • Essential cookies: needed for the site to work (for example, remembering form input).
  • Analytics cookies: to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Advertising cookies:primarily Meta's Pixel cookies (_fbp and _fbc), which help us measure the performance of our Facebook and Instagram ads.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of our site function.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, and as required by law. Typically:

  • Quote inquiries that don't proceed: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Active and completed customer records: kept for as long as the relationship is active and for up to 7 years after the last project for tax, warranty, and legal purposes.
  • Insurance claim records: kept for the period required by Ontario law and our insurer.

7. How we protect your information

We use reasonable physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. This includes encrypted storage, access controls, and trained staff. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using current industry practices.

8. Your rights and choices

Under PIPEDA and applicable Canadian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw consent for us to use your information, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Unsubscribe from any commercial messages we send you, at any time.
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you believe your rights have been violated.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details in section 11. We respond to verifiable requests within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses, property professionals, and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll provide a more prominent notice (for example, an email or homepage banner). Your continued use of our website or services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
